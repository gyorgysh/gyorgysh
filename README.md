### Gyorgy

Ten years of building software for real users, and running the projects around it, taught me that the first version is the easy part. Keeping it good for years is the real work. These days I put that into open-source tools I wish already existed, and I write about how they come together on [gyorgy.sh](https://gyorgy.sh).
